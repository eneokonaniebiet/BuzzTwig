# Platform modules

BuzzTwig is one product with feature modules, not separate apps.

Core:
- messaging and contacts
- groups, communities and channels
- voice/video calls
- stories and media
- notifications
- privacy, security and device/session management

Commerce:
- wallet and ledger
- gifts and creator earnings
- subscriptions and paid communities
- business accounts and future mini-apps

AI:
- assistant entry point
- future moderation, discovery and productivity features

Every money-moving operation must be backed by a verified provider event and an idempotent ledger entry. Ordinary messaging remains free.
